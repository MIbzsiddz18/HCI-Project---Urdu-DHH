SYNTHETIC DEMO ONLY — entirely fictional example; not collected from people and not research evidence.

This folder is an isolated demo fixture, not collected participant data.
It contains 8 fictional older Pakistani Deaf/Hard of Hearing profiles,
8 session JSON files, a session CSV, and 40 task rows. One fictional task
is incomplete to demonstrate that outcome in the interface/export.

All participant IDs start with SYNTH-DEMO-. Every session has
synthetic_demo_only=true and consent_confirmed=false. The app does not load
this folder as real sessions; its real exports remain in data/raw/ directly.
Do not cite, analyze, or present these invented responses as study findings.

Regenerate with:
  .venv\Scripts\python.exe study\generate_synthetic_demo.py
